Lift maintenance FAQs

Important details before you choose.

Answers based on KEN-JO's published support information. Confirm contract-specific matters directly with the company.

K​​EN-JO's support FAQ describes its Standard Maintenance Package as monthly lift maintenance to identify early symptoms of wear and tear, corrective actions to keep the lift operating efficiently, and 24-hour call-back service.
KEN-JO states that its comprehensive package includes preventive maintenance and 24-hour call-back service, and covers repairs and most part replacements that may be necessary during the contract period. Refer to the contract for exact coverage and exclusions.
KEN-JO describes the non-comprehensive option as covering preventive maintenance and 24-hour emergency call-back service. Parts replacement and repairs are not typically included in that package description. Ask for a written breakdown of any additional charges.
KEN-JO states that its maintenance focus is on maintaining products supplied by the company. Contact KEN-JO with your lift details to confirm whether your equipment is eligible for maintenance support.
KEN-JO's support page lists 6438 8763 as its 24-hour hotline for lift breakdowns or passenger entrapment.
Maintenance frequency depends on the lift and applicable requirements. KEN-JO's FAQ states that private residential lifts are required to be maintained at least every three months unless the manufacturer's advice requires a more frequent schedule. Confirm the applicable schedule with your lift contractor.
